FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Email hello@squaredmc.com

Is Netti free?

Yes. The calculator is free to download and use, with no ads. Netti Pro is an optional one-off purchase, £3.99 in the UK, that adds extra income, Compare and salary sacrifice.

What does Netti Pro unlock?

Three things. Extra income: a bonus, overtime, commission or a single payment, and how each is taxed. Compare: a job offer next to what you take home now. Salary sacrifice: what a Cycle to Work bike or an electric car through work really costs, after the tax and National Insurance saved. It is one payment, not a subscription.

Is my salary information private?

Yes. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open Netti and enter your salary.

Does Netti work for Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland?

Yes. Choose England, Wales and Northern Ireland, or Scotland, and Netti uses that region’s Income Tax bands.

Does Netti handle student loans and pensions?

Yes, in the free calculator. Student loan Plans 1, 2, 4 and 5 and Postgraduate, and pensions by salary sacrifice or relief at source.

Is Netti up to date with this tax year?

Netti uses HMRC’s 2026–27 rates and thresholds, and is updated when they change.

I’m moving to the UK. Can I use Netti?

Yes. Netti shows what a UK salary leaves you with after UK Income Tax, National Insurance and pension, so you can see it before you arrive. It works out UK tax only, not tax in any other country.

Is Netti available on Android or iPad?

Not yet. Netti is for iPhone, with iOS 17 or later. An Android version is being built.

Does Netti give financial or tax advice?

No. Netti gives estimates for guidance only. Your payslip may differ because of cumulative PAYE, tax code changes or how your employer runs payroll. Netti is not financial or tax advice.

How do I get support?

Email hello@squaredmc.com and we’ll reply.
